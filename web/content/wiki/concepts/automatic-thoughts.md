## Definition

**Automatic thoughts** are the quick sentences that flash through when something happens — often so fast they feel like facts:

- "They ignored me on purpose."
- "I'm going to fail."
- "This feeling means something is wrong."

They sit in the middle of the [cognitive model](/wiki/concepts/cognitive-model): **situation → automatic thought → emotion + body + behavior**.

## Why they matter

Change the **thought** (or your relationship to it), and emotion and behavior often shift — not because you "positive think," but because you test accuracy and usefulness with evidence.

## How Willow elicits them

One question at a time:

- "What went through your mind right then?"
- "When you noticed that in your body, what was the thought?"

Not multiple-choice — your words.
