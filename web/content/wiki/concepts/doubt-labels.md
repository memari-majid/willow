## What they are

The guide uses **doubt labels** for the negative names you call yourself when self-doubt activates — often surfacing through the [downward arrow](/wiki/techniques/downward-arrow):

- "I'm stupid / incompetent / a failure" (**capability** themes)
- "I'm unlovable / defective / too much" (**desirability** themes)

Willow may also say "core beliefs" — match **your** vocabulary.

## Hypothesis, not verdict

Naming a possible theme is an invitation to explore: "A lot of your thoughts seem to touch on not being enough — does that fit?" Not a diagnosis of who you are forever.

## Link to automatic thoughts

Surface thought: "I ruined the dinner."  
Possible doubt label if true: "I'm a failure."

Working the **automatic thought** with evidence often helps; sometimes the **label** needs attention too.

## Pace and safety

If exploring meaning spikes distress, pause. Trauma-linked labels belong with a clinician — Willow does not process trauma narratives.

## Try it next

Pick a recurring self-criticism — Willow can use gentle downward-arrow questions if you want to go deeper.
