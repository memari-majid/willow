## What it is

The thought record is the **workhorse** CBT exercise. You take one activating situation — not your whole week — and map how your mind and body responded, then look at evidence and choose a next step.

In the guide this culminates in **Go Time**:

- **Rethink** — a more accurate or helpful view given the facts.
- **Relax** — notice whether emotion intensity shifts (re-rate 0–10).
- **Respond** — one concrete action, however small.

## Steps (one at a time)

Willow asks these **one per turn** in conversation — not as a form to rush:

1. **Activating situation** — What specifically happened? Strip the story to the trigger.
2. **Body response** — What did you notice physically?
3. **Automatic thought** — What went through your mind? More than one is fine.
4. **Emotion (0–10)** — Name it and rate intensity.
5. **Thinking error (optional)** — Is there a pattern (catastrophizing, mind reading)? Naming helps; don't get stuck here.
6. **Doubt label (optional)** — If true, what would this thought say about you?
7. **Facts** — Evidence for and against; other explanations; what would someone who cares about you say?
8. **Rethink** — Given the facts, what's a more accurate or helpful way to see this?
9. **Relax** — Re-rate the emotion. Any shift?
10. **Respond** — What specific action will you take now?

If distress is high, Willow slows down further. You can pause anytime.

## Alternatives within the same spirit

When a full record is not the fit:

- **Best friend test** — What would you tell a friend who said this exact thought?
- **Continuum** — On a 0–100 scale, where does this situation actually land?
- **Pie chart** — What other factors contributed besides you?

## Scope

- This is **skill practice**, not trauma processing. Willow does not use the record to push you through traumatic memories.
- For [anxiety](/wiki/anxiety) tied to OCD/PTSD/panic, technique support stops short of exposure design without a clinician.

## Try it next

Open chat with a **single situation** from the last few days — even a 10-minute friction point is enough to start.
