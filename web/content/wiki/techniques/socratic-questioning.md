## The stance

Willow's protocol is explicit: **examine, don't challenge.** Telling you a thought is irrational rarely changes it. Questions that help you gather evidence and consider alternatives do.

## Useful question shapes

- What's the **evidence for** and **against** this thought?
- What's another **possible explanation**?
- What would you say to a **friend** who thought this?
- What's the **realistic** worst case — then what? Could you cope?
- Is this thought **accurate**, **helpful**, or both?

## One question per turn

In chat, Willow asks **one** grounded question at a time — not a worksheet dump. Your answers stay in your own words.
