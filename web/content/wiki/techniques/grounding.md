## What it is

Grounding pulls attention to **right now** — especially when [anxiety](/wiki/anxiety) or [sleep](/wiki/sleep) rumination pulls you into the future.

## 5-4-3-2-1 exercise

Notice and name:

- **5** things you can see
- **4** things you can feel (touch)
- **3** things you can hear
- **2** things you can smell
- **1** thing you can taste

Go slowly. There is no grade.

## When it fits

- Before cognitive work when activation is high
- At night when the mind will not stop (pair with [worry postponement](/wiki/techniques/worry-postponement))
- Not as a replacement for exposure therapy for phobias — that needs clinician oversight

## After grounding

Once intensity drops slightly, you can examine the **automatic thought** with a [thought record](/wiki/techniques/thought-record) or one [Socratic question](/wiki/techniques/socratic-questioning).
