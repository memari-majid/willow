## Core idea

Inactivity feeds low mood; action is the antidote — but you often have to **move first**, not wait to feel like it.

## Steps

1. **Brief activity log** — a few time blocks with mood 0–10. Notice patterns.
2. **P and A list** — activities that gave **P**leasure or **A**ccomplishment/mastery (keep items small).
3. **Schedule one** — what, when, where. Specific beats vague.
4. **Predict then compare** — rate expected mood after; compare to actual. Low mood often predicts "0"; reality is often higher.

## Pair with thought work

Activation changes behavior; [thought records](/wiki/techniques/thought-record) address the thoughts that justify staying in bed. Both matter for [low mood](/wiki/low-mood).

## Caution

If you have history of **manic or hypomanic episodes**, coordinate activation intensity with a clinician — too much too fast can destabilize.
