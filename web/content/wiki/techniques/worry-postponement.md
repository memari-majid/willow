## When to use it

Best for **generalized worry** — the same themes looping all day — rather than a single acute situation (use a [thought record](/wiki/techniques/thought-record) for that).

## Setup

1. Pick a **daily worry time** — same place, ~15–20 minutes, not right before sleep if possible.
2. When worry intrudes outside that window, **write a few words** on the topic and say you'll return at worry time.
3. At worry time, review the list. For each item: problem-solve if actionable, or use [Socratic questions](/wiki/techniques/socratic-questioning) if it's hypothetical.

## What it trains

Your brain learns worries have a **container** — reducing the habit of scanning for threat every hour.

## Limits

Not a substitute for treatment of GAD, OCD, or trauma-related rumination. See [Worry](/wiki/worry) for when to involve a clinician.
