## If you are in crisis now

**Talking to a real human matters more than reading or chatting.**

### United States

- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** — call or text **988**, 24/7.
- **Crisis Text Line** — text **HOME** to **741741**.
- **Veterans Crisis Line** — dial **988**, then press **1**, or text **838255**.

### United Kingdom & Ireland

- **Samaritans** — **116 123**, free, 24/7.

### Canada

- **Talk Suicide Canada** — **1-833-456-4566** or text **45645** (4 PM – midnight ET).

### Australia

- **Lifeline** — **13 11 14**, 24/7.

### International

- [Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com) — directory by country.
- [IASP crisis centres](https://www.iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres/).

### Immediate physical danger

Call local emergency services — **911** (US/Canada), **999** (UK), **112** (EU), **000** (Australia).

## What Willow does when crisis language appears

Before the main CBT model runs, Willow prescreens messages for crisis signals. If you mention suicidal thoughts, self-harm, or similar:

1. Willow **acknowledges** what you said calmly and directly.
2. It **surfaces human resources** (like those above).
3. It **stays in the conversation** — it does not require you to call before continuing to talk.
4. It does **not** list means, promise confidentiality, or dramatize the moment.

This wiki page is informational. In chat, the crisis pathway overrides normal technique guidance.

## Boundaries — wiki and Willow

Neither this wiki nor Willow:

- Diagnoses mental health conditions.
- Recommends or comments on medications.
- Provides medical, legal, or financial advice.
- Replaces therapy, psychiatry, or emergency services.
- Promises outcomes ("you will feel better").

Learning pages about [anxiety](/wiki/anxiety) or [low mood](/wiki/low-mood) are **not** invitations to do unsupervised exposure or trauma processing. Those belong with trained clinicians.

## When to see a clinician

Consider professional care when:

- Symptoms persist for weeks and limit daily life.
- You have thoughts of harming yourself or others.
- You rely on alcohol, drugs, or self-harm to cope.
- You hear or see things others do not, or lose track of reality.
- You have trauma, eating-disorder behaviors, or bipolar history that affects treatment choices.

Willow can help you **prepare questions** for a clinician; it cannot replace their judgment.

## Memory and privacy in chat

Willow may remember facts you share across conversations (manage in **Settings → Memory**). After elevated safety concern, memory writes may be blocked for that turn. You can delete stored memories anytime.
