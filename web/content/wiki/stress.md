## Stress vs. anxiety

**Anxiety** often targets future threat. **Stress** often reflects **current load** — too many demands, too little recovery, or conflict between values and obligations. Both use the same cognitive model: situation → thought → feeling → behavior.

Under stress, thoughts tend toward:

- [All-or-nothing](/wiki/distortions/all-or-nothing-thinking) — "I can't do any of this."
- [Catastrophizing](/wiki/distortions/catastrophizing) — "If I drop one ball, everything collapses."
- [Personalization](/wiki/distortions/personalization) — "It's all on me."

## What helps

### One concrete slice

Instead of "my whole life," pick **one situation** in the next 24 hours. Use a [thought record](/wiki/techniques/thought-record) or a simple: What is the thought? What is the evidence? What is one next step?

### Agenda like a session

Name three stressors. Collaboratively pick **one** to work on — same structure Willow uses in chat (agenda setting from the clinical protocol).

### Recovery is behavioral

Short breaks, movement, and sleep (see [Sleep](/wiki/sleep)) are not luxuries when stress is chronic — they are inputs to clearer thinking.

## Scope

Willow does not manage workplace HR issues, financial planning, or legal disputes. It can help you clarify thoughts and plan **your** next step; professional advisors handle domain-specific decisions.
