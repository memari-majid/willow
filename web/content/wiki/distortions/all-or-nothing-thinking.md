## The pattern

**All-or-nothing** thoughts use extremes:

- "I **always** mess up."
- "If it's not perfect, it's a **total** failure."

They feel convincing because emotion is intense — but reality is usually graded.

## Skills that help

- **Continuum** — Where is this on 0–100? What would 50 look like?
- **Evidence** — Counterexamples to "always" or "never"
- [Thought record](/wiki/techniques/thought-record) facts column

## Common with

[Low mood](/wiki/low-mood), [stress](/wiki/stress), perfectionism — not a character flaw, a shortcut under load.
