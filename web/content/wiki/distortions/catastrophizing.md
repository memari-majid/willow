## The pattern

**Catastrophizing** magnifies how bad an outcome would be and often pairs with [probability errors](/wiki/anxiety): "If this goes wrong, my life is over."

## Questions that help

- What's the **realistic** worst case — not the movie version?
- Then what happens next?
- Have you survived similar setbacks before?
- What resources or people could you use?
