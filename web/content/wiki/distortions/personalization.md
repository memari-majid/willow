## The pattern

**Personalization** assigns you undue blame:

- "The team failed because of me."
- "Their bad mood must be my fault."

Sometimes you **did** contribute — CBT asks for **proportion**, not zero self-accountability.

## Pie chart technique

List **all factors** (timing, others' choices, context, luck). Assign rough percentages. Total responsibility rarely stays at 100% on one person.

## Pair with

[Thought record](/wiki/techniques/thought-record) evidence step; [all-or-nothing](/wiki/distortions/all-or-nothing-thinking) when blame is total.
