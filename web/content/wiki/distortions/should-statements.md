## The pattern

**Shoulds** impose rules:

- "I **should** be further along by now."
- "They **should** have known better."
- "The world **shouldn't** be this unfair."

When reality violates the rule, anger or guilt spikes.

## Should → preference

"I should be on time" → "I'd **prefer** to be on time; if I'm late, I can **still** …"

See [Anger](/wiki/anger) for assertive next steps when someone else violates your preference.

## Self-shoulds and mood

Common in [stress](/wiki/stress) and [low mood](/wiki/low-mood) — perfectionistic rules that ignore constraints.
