## The pattern

**Mind reading** = treating assumptions about others' thoughts as facts:

- "They think I'm incompetent."
- "Everyone noticed I was awkward."

## Examine with evidence

- What did they **actually say or do**?
- What are **other explanations** for their behavior?
- Have you ever been wrong about what someone thought before?
- Would you need to **ask** to know — and is asking safe/possible?

## Social anxiety link

Common in [anxiety](/wiki/anxiety) before or after social situations. Pair with [Socratic questioning](/wiki/techniques/socratic-questioning).
