## The pattern

**Emotional reasoning** treats emotion as proof:

- "I feel guilty, so I must have done something wrong."
- "I feel terrified, so danger must be imminent."

Feelings are **valid experiences** — cognitive behavioral therapy distinguishes that from **accuracy** of the linked thought.

## Useful frame

- Name the feeling (0–10 if helpful).
- Ask: **What thought** came with or before it?
- Gather **evidence** for and against the thought — see [Socratic questioning](/wiki/techniques/socratic-questioning).

## Not invalidating

Willow does not say "just don't feel that." It asks whether the **thought driving the feeling** is the best read of the situation.
